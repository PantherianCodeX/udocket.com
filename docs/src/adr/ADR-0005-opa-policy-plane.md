# ADR-0005 — Open Policy Agent policy plane for residency & HIPAA enforcement

- **Status:** Accepted  
- **Date:** 2025-10-12  
- **Deciders:** Security Review Board, Architecture Steering Committee, Platform SRE Lead  
- **Tags:** opa, policy, residency, hipaa, bundles

## Context

Settings/LPE provide deterministic data, but we lacked a hardened policy execution environment. Inline policy code in services led to:

- Divergent enforcement between Guardian, portal downloads, and workers.  
- Difficulty auditing residency/HIPAA decisions.  
- Risk of regressions when updating residency allowlists or attachment rules.  
- No ready-made decision logs or bundle health visibility.

We needed a shared, verifiable policy engine that can evaluate residency, HIPAA overrides, attachment routing, and egress in real time while consuming the signed bundles produced by RM/LPE.

## Decision

Adopt Open Policy Agent (OPA) as the policy plane.

- **Deployment**: Operate OPA as a sidecar for latency-sensitive services (web, workers, Guardian) and as a shared control-plane pool for background tasks. Each instance authenticates via mTLS and presents a service identity.  
- **Bundle distribution**: RM publishes Rego + JSON bundles (signed with Ed25519). Discovery manifests (`/api/v1/lpe/opa/discovery`) advertise bundle URIs, digests, rollout channels, and minimum versions. OPA enforces signature verification and fails closed on mismatch/staleness.  
- **Policies**: Residency/egress allowlists, HIPAA overrides, attachment routing, and waiver logic live in `rego` modules under `packages/udocket_core/opa`. Policies read dataset projections generated by LPE (organizations, privacy flags, region allowlists).  
- **Decision APIs**: services query endpoints such as `/v1/data/udocket/residency/allow` or embed OPA evaluations via Wasm for tight loops. Standard input includes `org_id`, `case_id`, `artifact_type`, `requested_region`, and settings snapshot hash.  
- **Observability**: Enable decision logs (scrubbed of PII) and status logs. Metrics (`opa_decision_latency_seconds`, `opa_bundle_status{state}`, `opa_denied_total`) feed dashboards/alerts.  
- **Failure mode**: Residency, HIPAA, and attachment decisions fail closed. Services treat OPA timeouts/errors as policy blocks and surface actionable errors to operators; alerts trigger runbooks.

## Consequences

- Policy updates roll out by publishing new signed bundles—no service restarts.  
- We gain tamper-evident decision history, bundle health monitoring, and consistent enforcement semantics.  
- Additional operational responsibilities: bundle key rotation, OPA upgrade cadence, decision-log retention, and Wasm compatibility tests.  
- Engineers must write policy in Rego and maintain regression tests (`opa test`). We provide helper libraries to marshal inputs/outputs.  
- System complexity increases (OPA bootstrap, discovery, caches), but the payoff is stronger compliance guarantees and simplified audits.
