# uDocket Platform System Overview

## Purpose
This document consolidates the current uDocket platform description, clarifies module boundaries, and highlights missing subsystems that must exist for a complete implementation. It supersedes conflicting descriptions elsewhere in the repository.

## Platform Foundation
- **Technology stack**: Django 5.2, Channels, Celery, LangGraph agents, Python 3.12.
- **Multi-tenancy**: Organization-scoped data segregation with per-org settings, roles, and retention policies.
- **Deployment**: Containerized services orchestrated through Docker Compose; Azure Canada regions for AI workloads and storage.
- **Storage root**: `storage/media/cases/<CASE_ID>/` with `audio/`, `transcript/`, `analysis/`, `docs/`, and `ops/` directories.
- **Version control for files**: Immutable artifact history with SHA-256 hashing and quarantine enforcement.
- **Observability**: Structured logs, metrics, and audit trails across all services.

## Workflow Modules (Steps 1–10)
### Step 1 – Intake & Case Initialization
- **Intake Module**: Collects client, party, jurisdiction, and court metadata via adaptive forms; enforces org policies and validation rules.
- **Case & Party Registry**: Manages case creation, participant roles, and relational data; ensures multi-tenant isolation.
- **Reference System**: Courthouse catalog (JSON-backed transitioning to DB), jurisdictional naming, validation profiles, and field-level Pydantic v2 validators exposed through a validation engine API.
- **Document ID & Validation Service**: Responds to validation queries for case/document identifiers using reference data.

### Step 2 – Interview & Questionnaire Preparation
- **Questionnaire Module**: Self-contained lifecycle for question banks, generation, editing, versioning, and scheduled self-improvement tasks (daily/weekly cadence). Supports AI-assisted customization before interviews.
- **Interview Hub**: Presents case context, questionnaire checklist, and live notes; integrates AI prompt adjustments and tracks interviewer progress.
- **Pre-Interview AI Adjuster**: Optional LangGraph-powered assistant to tailor questionnaires based on intake metadata.

### Step 3 – Transcription Module
- **Audio Intake & Metadata Service**: Uses `ffprobe` to capture media metadata upon upload.
- **Format Normalizer**: Converts non-compliant audio (Azure Batch preferred format) via `ffmpeg`, records conversion subtasks, and re-runs metadata extraction.
- **Transcription Pipeline**: LangGraph module (renamed from agent) driving Azure Batch or on-demand transcription with diarization support (batch mode).
- **Websocket Telemetry**: Streams job progress and conversion status to the UI.
- **Versioning & SHA-256 Hashing Module**: Applies strict immutable versioning, hashing on write/read, quarantine for mismatches, and exposes validation/quarantine APIs.

### Step 4 – Analyze Module
- **Structured Extraction Pipelines**: Parallel lanes for timeline events, issues, entities, facts, intake data alignment, and raw transcript packaging, each with QA loops.
- **Gap Detection Engine**: Consolidates inconsistencies, missing data, and documentation requirements.
- **Roadmap Enhancements**: Future ingestion of exhibits, court documents, financials, staff notes, and email evidence with AI-based fact checking.
- **Review Hooks**: Produces artifacts pending reviewer approval before downstream use.

### Step 5 – Compose Module
- **LangGraph Composition Pipelines**: Separate client and lawyer document lanes, each delivering Markdown and DOCX outputs.
- **Quality Gates**: Automated structure, policy compliance, and factuality checks gating completion.
- **Template Manager**: Selects organization-specific document templates with fallback defaults.

### Step 6 – Document Assembly
- **Template Substitution Engine**: Injects Compose outputs into organization templates in parallel for client and lawyer deliverables.
- **Artifact Promotion**: Wraps outputs with metadata via the Artifacts Module and routes to Guardian for final approval.

### Step 7 – Final Review
- **Staff Review Workspace**: Central UI for reviewers to inspect artifacts, request corrections, and approve releases; integrates diffing and audio playback for references.

### Step 8 – Document Delivery
- **Delivery Coordinator**: Routes approved documents to clients and representatives through secure channels, logs acknowledgements, and respects notification preferences.
- **Roadmap**: Integration with future client portal and automated outreach workflows.

### Step 9 – Factuality Corrections Loop
- **Correction Intake Module**: Captures client factual corrections, re-injects them into Analyze pipelines, and orchestrates re-runs through Compose and delivery.
- **Iteration Tracker**: Maintains loop counts, timestamps, and approvals for auditability.

### Step 10 – Data Destruction
- **Data Destruction Module**: Enforces retention schedules, processes early destruction requests, issues destruction certificates, and validates completion.
- **Periodic Audit Jobs**: Detect and purge orphaned or expired data, synchronizing with audit trail expiration rules.

## Cross-Cutting Modules & Subsystems
- **Artifacts Module**: Governs artifact creation, metadata, storage, lifecycle tracking, destruction workflows, and reviewer integrations.
- **Manual Edit Tool**: Embedded Markdown editor producing versioned proposals with fact-change validation and reviewer workflows.
- **AI Edit Tool**: LangGraph chatbot assisting edits under the same validation and approval constraints as manual edits.
- **Review Module**: Handles review request routing, notifications, escalation policies, and reviewer workload tracking.
- **Guardian Sub-System**: Final policy enforcement authority scanning artifacts, monitoring for violations, managing quarantines, enforcing demotions/escalations, and running watchdog health checks.
- **Audit Sub-System**: Append-only audit of all actions (including superusers), configurable retention aligned with destruction policies; integrates Guardian tamper detection and optional watchdogs.
- **Logging Module**: Central logging pipeline with structured events, correlation IDs, and reporting; feeds observability stack.
- **Settings Module**: Unified configuration surface spanning Django, LangGraph, UI, and per-organization overrides.
- **Security & Compliance**: RBAC/ABAC authorization, multi-tenant isolation, secret management, encryption at rest/in transit, jurisdictional residency (Canada regions), and policy engines for Guardian.
- **Authentication & Identity**: Supports staff SSO, MFA, session management, and service accounts; ties into organization onboarding.
- **Case Management & Workflow Orchestration**: Tracks job states, Celery tasks, dependency chains, and exposes APIs for UI and automation.
- **Notifications & Communications**: Email, in-app, and webhook notifications for job status, reviews, Guardian alerts, and client deliveries; respects org-level preferences.
- **Task Scheduling**: Cron-like scheduler for questionnaire self-improvement, periodic audits, Guardian scans, data destruction, and health checks.
- **Storage & Hash Registry**: Maintains version metadata, hash registries, and quarantine state across all file types.
- **Analytics & Reporting**: Dashboards for throughput, review metrics, compliance KPIs, and audit queries; integrates with Guardian and Audit outputs.
- **Client Portal (Roadmap)**: Future-facing interface for clients to approve staff notes, review documents, request corrections, and manage destruction requests.
- **Integration Connectors**: API surface for external services (e.g., court e-filing, CRM, billing) with governance controls and audit hooks.
- **Developer Tooling**: Local docker environment, typing enforcement, automated tests, and scripts supporting consistent deployments.

## Naming Conventions & Versioning Notes
- Refer to agents as modules within the UI while retaining LangGraph agent terminology internally when required.
- File artifacts use `<job_id>__<artifact>_v<version>.<ext>` naming; never overwrite previous versions.
- All structured records include stable UUIDs; use deterministic UUIDv5 when regenerating content.
- Hash failures result in immediate quarantine and require Guardian clearance before reactivation.

## Governance & Roadmap Alignment
- Align future work with the roadmap to complete missing document types in Analyze, client portal integration, full automation of reviewer roles, and Django 5.2 migration.
- Ensure every module exports validation helpers and APIs necessary for orchestration and third-party integrations.
- Guardian and Audit subsystems are non-optional gatekeepers; all new modules must register events, hashes, and policy decisions with them.
